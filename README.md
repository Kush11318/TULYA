# 🔍 PriceLens  
### Focus on value. Blur the noise.

PriceLens is a modern price-comparison platform designed to surface **what actually matters** when buying online — clarity, relevance, and value.

Instead of relying on keyword-heavy scraping or paid APIs, PriceLens uses a real browser-based engine to understand product context, normalize listings, and present prices in a calm, minimal interface inspired by modern product dashboards.

---

## ✨ Core Capabilities

### 🔎 Semantic Product Matching  
Understands product context and normalizes listings to the actual product model, reducing keyword noise and peripheral results.

### 💸 Live Multi-Source Price Comparison  
Aggregates real-time prices from multiple marketplaces and presents them in a unified, comparable format.

### ⭐ Ratings & Review Snapshot  
Surfaces available star ratings and review counts to provide quick confidence signals.

### 📊 Price History & Trends  
Tracks historical price points and visualizes trends to help identify the best time to buy.

### 🏷️ Best Price & Best Value Highlighting  
Automatically identifies the lowest price and highlights value-driven options.

### 🧮 Advanced Comparison Table  
Compare multiple products side-by-side with structured attributes and key differences.

### 🎨 Minimal Interface with Micro-Interactions  
Clean typography, subtle color grading, and restrained animations for a distraction-free experience.

### 🚫 No APIs. No Quotas.  
Powered by a Puppeteer-based backend — no third-party scraping APIs, no usage limits.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. A product query is submitted  
2. A headless browser loads real marketplace pages  
3. Listings are extracted, cleaned, and normalized  
4. Results are deduplicated, scored, and grouped  
5. The most relevant options are displayed with clarity

---

## 🛠 Technology Stack

- Next.js (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui
- MagicUI (Warp Background)
- Puppeteer + Stealth Plugin
- Recharts (Price Visualization)

---

## 📍 Design Philosophy

PriceLens is built around three principles:

- **Relevance over volume**  
- **Clarity over clutter**  
- **Insight over information**

Every visual and interaction exists to reduce friction and highlight value.
